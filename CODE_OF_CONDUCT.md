# Code of conduct

AgentLoopGate contributors are expected to make technical collaboration safe,
respectful, and evidence-driven.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect across differences in experience, identity,
  language, discipline, and viewpoint.
- Critique claims and implementations with concrete evidence, without personal
  attacks or harassment.
- Give credit, disclose conflicts of interest, and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private data, credentials, unpublished evidence, and responsible
  vulnerability reports.
- Accept that a well-supported `HOLD` or negative result is valuable project
  evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation of evidence, retaliation, and unauthorized disclosure of
private project material are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository Owner. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and in good faith. Proportionate responses may include
a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion
from project spaces. The Owner will avoid conflicts of interest when handling a
report and will seek another trusted maintainer when necessary.
